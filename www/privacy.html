Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Duttarim is a musical instrument tuning application designed for the dutar. This Privacy Policy explains how the application uses device permissions and handles information while you use the app.

Microphone Access

Duttarim requires access to your device's microphone in order to detect the sound produced by your dutar and determine its pitch.

Microphone audio is processed in real time on your device for tuning purposes. Duttarim does not intentionally record, store, upload, or share your microphone audio.

Information We Collect

Duttarim does not require you to create an account and does not currently collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

Local App Preferences

Some preferences may be stored locally on your device so that the application can remember your settings. These may include:

  • Selected application language
  • Note notation preference
  • Reference tuning frequency
  • Whether the initial setup has been completed
  • Favorite songs
  • Cached song catalog and new-song status

These preferences are stored locally on your device and are used only to provide the application's features and remember your settings.

Internet Connection

The core tuning functionality is designed to process audio locally on your device. An internet connection is not required for the microphone pitch analysis itself.

When an internet connection is available, Duttarim checks its official online catalog for new or updated sheet music. Catalog information and sheet images are downloaded from the official Duttarim GitHub Pages content address. A previously downloaded catalog may be cached on your device for offline use.

New-Song Notifications

Duttarim compares the online catalog with identifiers stored locally on your device. This allows the app to show a “New” label and an in-app notification when new sheet music becomes available. Opening a new song clears its local unread status. The app does not send your favorites or reading history to the catalog service.

Score Management

The application includes a restricted score-management interface intended for the developer or an authorized administrator. When that interface is used, the entered song details and selected score images are sent over an encrypted connection to the official Duttarim publishing service hosted on Cloudflare. The service publishes approved content to the official Duttarim GitHub repository.

The administrator access code is used only to authorize the publishing request and is not included in the public song catalog. Administrators should keep this code confidential.

Service Providers and Technical Data

Duttarim uses GitHub Pages to distribute its online song catalog and Cloudflare Workers to protect the restricted publishing service. These providers may process limited technical information, such as IP address, request time, device or browser information, and diagnostic logs, according to their own privacy and security practices.

Data Sharing

Duttarim does not sell personal information. Limited technical data may be processed by the service providers described above only to deliver, secure, and maintain the online catalog and publishing features.

Children's Privacy

Duttarim does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the application does not currently require an account or collect personal information through its core tuning functionality, no personal profile is created for users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when new features or services are added to Duttarim. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer through the official Duttarim application or its official distribution page.
