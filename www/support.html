Microphone and tuning

Duttarim needs microphone permission to hear your dutar and identify its pitch. Allow microphone access in your device settings, then reopen the tuner. Microphone audio is processed on your device and is not saved or uploaded.

Sheet music catalog

An internet connection is needed to check for new or updated sheet music. Previously available local or cached content may remain accessible without a connection.

Settings and favorites

Language, notation, tuning preferences, favorites, and similar settings are stored locally on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes these local preferences.

Still need help?

Email duttarim.app@gmail.com. Please describe what happened, your device model, and your operating-system version. Do not send audio recordings or sensitive personal information.
